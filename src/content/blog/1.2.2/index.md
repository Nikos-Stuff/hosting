## Streamlined Free Tier Verification

We're replacing required 3DS card verification on our Free Tier with automated, background activity checks.

**What's changing:**

- **No friction:** New Free Tier users no longer need to complete a 3DS card check during sign-up.
- **Dynamic security:** Verification now happens seamlessly in the background based on behavioral patterns.
- **Risk-based triggers:** Standard 3DS checks will only be triggered if unusual or suspicious activity is detected.

Enjoy a faster, hassle-free onboarding process!
