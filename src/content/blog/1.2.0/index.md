# Free Tier Update 
We’ve officially completed a full purge of suspicious accounts on our free tier to keep things safe, stable, and abuse-free for everyone. :tennacabbagepatch: 

## What changed:
- **Card Verification Required:** Moving forward, claiming a free tier slot requires a quick card setup ($0 charge) to verify you're a real user and keep bad actors out.
- **100% Secure & Private:** All billing details are handled directly by Stripe-we never see or store your private info.
- **Better Reliability:** Fewer abusers hogging resources means better overall performance and stability for genuine users.

That's it for now.  See you soon!
